Data from 2016 was excluded from the analysis. Olist operations began late in 2016, making this year incomplete and unsuitable for reliable demand estimation. Including partial-year data could bias demand and capacity estimates downward.

Only data from 2017 and 2018 was retained to ensure consistency and reliability.
