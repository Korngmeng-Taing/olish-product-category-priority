# 1. Introduction
**Olist** is a leading e-commerce marketplace in Brazil connecting small businesses to customers. However, logistics networks have breaking points.

### The Business Challenge
If Olist fills trucks with heavy, cheap items (like cement), they lose money. If they fill them with light, expensive items (like watches), they maximize profit. The challenge is finding the **mathematical balance** between volume, weight, and price.

::: {.callout-important appearance="simple"}
## Research Question
**Which product categories should Olist prioritize to maximize total revenue, given the strict constraints on historical handling capacity?**
:::
:::

::: {.data-dictionary}
### <i class="bi bi-diagram-3"></i> Variable Mapping

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Decision Variable | $x_i$ | Quantity of  item for category i  |  product_category_name|
| Objective coefficient| $r_i$ | average revenue per unit for category i | $r_i= \frac{\text{price in category i}}{\text{number item sold in category i}}$ |
| Constraint Parameter | $D_i$ | Demand  linit for category i| $D_i= \frac{\text{total item sold in category i}}{total month}$ |

# 5. Mathematical Formulation

We model this as a **Resource Allocation Problem** (Knapsack-style variant).

<!-- CHANGED FROM ::: TO HTML DIV TO PREVENT ERRORS -->
<div class="math-model-card">

<!-- HEADER -->
<h3><i class="bi bi-calculator"></i> The Linear Program</h3>


<!-- 1. OBJECTIVE -->
<div class="math-row">
  <div class="math-label">1. Objective Function</div>
  <div class="math-desc">
    Maximize total revenue ($Z$) using Price ($r_i$) and Quantity ($x_i$).
  </div>
  <div class="eqn-box">
    $$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i }(r_i \cdot x_i) $$
  </div>
</div>

<!-- 2. CAPACITY -->
<div class="math-row">
  <div class="math-label">2. Capacity Constraint</div>
  <div class="math-desc">
    The total quantity cannot exceed the limit ($C$).
  </div>
  <div class="eqn-box">
    $$ \sum_{i } x_i \leq C $$
  </div>
</div>

<!-- 3. DEMAND -->
<div class="math-row">
  <div class="math-label">3. Demand Constraints</div>
  <div class="math-desc">
    We cannot sell more than the historical market demand ($D_i$).
  </div>
  <div class="eqn-box">
    $$ x_i \leq D_i \quad \forall i  $$
  </div>
</div>

<!-- 4. NON-NEGATIVITY -->
<div class="math-row" style="border-bottom: none;">
  <div class="math-label">4. Non-Negativity</div>
  <div class="math-desc">
    Quantities must be positive integers.
  </div>
  <div class="eqn-box">
    $$ x_i \geq 0 $$

\[ x_i \leq D_i \quad \forall i \]
  1. Non-Negativity

\[ x_i \geq 0 \]
